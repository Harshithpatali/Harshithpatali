## Featured projects

Four flagship projects across statistical decision science, customer analytics, causal modelling and forecasting. Each README documents methodology, evaluation evidence and limitations.

### 01 · Causal Uplift Modelling for Email Marketing

**Randomized experiment → heterogeneous treatment effects → targeting policy evaluation**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Harshithpatali/causal-uplift-marketing-optimization)

- 64,000-customer Hillstrom randomized email experiment.
- ATE analysis and T-Learner / class-transformation uplift models.
- Held-out Qini, AUUC and uplift-at-depth evaluation.
- Best reported AUUC 0.002054 and Qini 0.004129.

**Stack:** Python · Pandas · LightGBM · Causal inference · Statistical evaluation

<sub>Policy estimates are evaluation-sample results, not guaranteed future campaign outcomes; the dataset does not include campaign costs for net-profit measurement.</sub>

### 02 · Customer Intelligence & Graph Anomaly Research

**Customer behaviour, segmentation, anomaly investigation, and entity networks**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Harshithpatali/Graph-anomaly-supervised-neural-prediction) [![Live app](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-Dashboard-FF4B4B?style=flat-square&logo=streamlit&logoColor=white)](https://graph-anomaly-supervised-neural-prediction-v1.streamlit.app/)

- Customer-level behavioural profiles and unsupervised segmentation.
- Isolation Forest and Local Outlier Factor anomaly ranking.
- User–device–IP graph exploration, connectivity measures, and communities.
- Customer 360 investigation with supporting behavioural evidence.

**Stack:** Python · Pandas · scikit-learn · NetworkX · Plotly · Streamlit

<sub>Research note: behavioural-risk scores are cohort-relative heuristics, not calibrated fraud probabilities. Anomaly rankings are investigation aids, not proof of wrongdoing.</sub>

### 03 · Brazilian E-Commerce Intelligence

**SQL-first marketplace analytics and customer decision science**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Harshithpatali/brazilian-ecommerce-data-science) [![Live app](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-Dashboard-FF4B4B?style=flat-square&logo=streamlit&logoColor=white)](https://olist-ecommerce-ds.streamlit.app/)

- Relational data modeling and analytical SQL transformations.
- RFM segmentation, customer value, cohorts, and retention analysis.
- Kaplan–Meier time-to-second-purchase analysis with right-censoring.
- Point-in-time churn research and baseline comparisons.
- Campaign scenario simulator linking assumptions to contribution economics.

**Stack:** Python · PostgreSQL · SQL · scikit-learn · MLflow · Streamlit

<sub>Model transparency: the documented current churn model did not outperform the lifetime-frequency baseline on the untouched holdout. The repository reports this limitation rather than overstating predictive performance.</sub>

### 04 · Retail Demand Forecasting & Inventory Optimization

**Forecasting connected to inventory cost and service-level decisions**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Harshithpatali/retail-demand-inventory-optimization) [![Dashboard](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-Dashboard-FF4B4B?style=flat-square&logo=streamlit&logoColor=white)](https://retail-demand-inventory-optimization-v1.streamlit.app/) [![API](https://img.shields.io/badge/API-FastAPI-009688?style=flat-square&logo=fastapi&logoColor=white)](https://retail-demand-inventory-optimization.onrender.com/)

- Naive, seasonal-naive, and moving-average baselines compared with XGBoost and LightGBM.
- Chronological validation and out-of-sample uncertainty estimation.
- Periodic-review inventory policy and simulation.
- Business evaluation through holding, ordering, and stockout costs.

**Documented validation result:** XGBoost WAPE 0.2808 versus 0.6873 for the naive baseline on the 200-series modeling subset.

**Stack:** Python · Pandas · XGBoost · LightGBM · FastAPI · Streamlit
